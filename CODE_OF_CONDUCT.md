# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, professional, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate constructively and assume good intent.
- Challenge ideas without attacking people.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Give actionable feedback supported by evidence.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property.
- Keep examples organization-neutral and safe for public use.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publication of confidential information, or repeated bad-faith participation are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when behaviour conflicts with this policy.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact channel listed in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted from widely used open-source community conduct principles and is intentionally concise for this documentation-focused project.
