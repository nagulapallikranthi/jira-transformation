# Sprint Governance Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook defines the governance model for sprint planning, execution, monitoring, reviews, retrospectives, reporting, risk management, dependencies, metrics, executive visibility, and continuous improvement across engineering teams.

## Sections
- Roles & responsibilities
- Sprint ceremonies
- Definition of Ready / Done
- Capacity planning
- Commitment management
- Scope change governance
- Dependency management
- Risk escalation
- Delivery metrics
- Executive dashboards
- Acceptance criteria
