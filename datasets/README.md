# Synthetic Datasets

This folder is reserved for fictional datasets that support dashboard demonstrations, tutorials, tests, and downloadable examples.

## Rules

- Use invented organizations, projects, teams, people, services, and financial values.
- Do not transform or anonymize real employer or customer exports for publication.
- Do not include live Jira keys, account IDs, field IDs, tenant names, URLs, email addresses, or incident details.
- Document schema, data types, generation logic, relationships, and known limitations.
- Include a data dictionary and reconciliation totals for every published dataset.

## Planned baseline datasets

- portfolio and program health;
- sprint commitments and completion;
- JSM incidents, requests, changes, and SLA events;
- deployment and reliability events;
- cloud-cost opportunities and realized savings;
- governance exceptions and adoption signals.

Synthetic data is illustrative and must not be presented as a benchmark or production outcome.
